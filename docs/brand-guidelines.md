## 3. Brand Voice & Editorial Standards

- **Privacy-First:** Always emphasize client-side browser execution and zero data retention in memory.
- **Accessible & Free:** 100% free with no registration, no watermarks, and no artificial paywalls.
- **Authentic Feedback:** Genuine community reviews only; no artificial ratings.
- **Clarity & Speed:** Clean 2-column layouts, instant live recalculation, and zero fluff.
